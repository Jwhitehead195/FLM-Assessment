Docusign Manager Skills Assessment

Baseline Your Front-Line Leadership Excellence

Why This Assessment?

Front-line managers are the backbone of sales execution. This assessment measures the seven core competencies that separate good managers from exceptional leaders—the behaviors that drive team performance, forecast accuracy, and sustainable revenue growth.

What You'll Discover

  • Business Ownership: Do you own the full P&L impact, or react to what comes?
  • Operating Rhythm: Are you protecting time for what matters, or drowning in urgency?
  • Coaching for Ownership: Are you developing reps or solving all their problems?
  • Deal Inspection: How rigorous is your qualification process?
  • Forecast Accuracy: Can leadership trust your numbers?
  • Retention & Expansion: Are you driving post-sale growth?
  • Financial Acumen: Do you connect daily decisions to business outcomes?

How It Works

You'll answer 21 scenario-based questions designed to reveal how you actually think and decide—not just what you know. No right answers. Your authentic responses surface where you're strong and where targeted development will unlock the next level of leadership impact.

What Comes Next

You'll receive a detailed report with your proficiency level (Advanced / Proficient / Developing) for each competency, specific insights into your strengths and growth areas, and practical context to drive your development. Your manager will also see aggregated insights across the team.

This takes about 10 minutes. Let's get started.
